# Contoso Field Engineering Handbook: Field Safety

## Personal Protective Equipment

Hard hats, safety glasses and steel-toed boots are mandatory at every customer site. High-visibility vests are required whenever vehicles are moving on site. Hearing protection is required inside any plant room that exceeds 85 decibels.

## Lone Worker Check-ins

Engineers working alone must check in with the dispatch desk every 2 hours. If a check-in is missed by more than 15 minutes, dispatch calls the engineer, and then the customer site contact. If neither can be reached within 30 minutes, dispatch contacts emergency services.

## Lockout and Tagout

Before servicing any pump, motor or valve actuator, the engineer must apply their personal lock and tag to the isolation point. Only the person who applied a lock may remove it. A shared lock box may be used for team jobs, but each engineer still attaches their own lock.

## Heat Stress

When the ambient temperature exceeds 35 degrees Celsius, outdoor work must be limited to 45 minutes of work followed by 15 minutes of rest in shade, and each engineer must drink at least 250 millilitres of water every 20 minutes.

## Battery Handling

Lithium battery packs must be stored in the fireproof cabinet in the service vehicle and never left in direct sunlight. Damaged or swollen packs must be reported to the safety officer and must not be transported in a passenger cabin.
