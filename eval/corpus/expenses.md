# Contoso Field Engineering Handbook: Travel and Expenses

## Per Diem and Meals

Field engineers on overnight assignments receive a meal per diem of $75 per day. The per diem covers breakfast, lunch and dinner and is paid without receipts. Alcohol is never reimbursable, and it cannot be claimed against the per diem.

## Lodging

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $180 per night in standard cities and up to $260 per night in San Francisco, New York and London. Engineers must book through the Contoso travel portal. Stays longer than 14 consecutive nights require approval from the regional director because an apartment lease is usually cheaper.

## Mileage and Rental Cars

Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile. Rental cars should be the compact or midsize class unless the engineer is transporting equipment that does not fit. Fuel for rental cars is reimbursed with a receipt; do not claim mileage and fuel for the same trip.

## Submitting Expense Reports

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip. Any single receipt over $25 must be attached, except for per diem meals. Reports containing a single item over $1,000 need written approval from a director before finance will process them. Late reports submitted after 60 days are rejected and cannot be reimbursed.
