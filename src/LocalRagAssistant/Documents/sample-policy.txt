Acme Outdoor Gear — Customer Policy Handbook

1. Refund Policy
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of the original purchase date, provided the
item is unused and in its original packaging. After 30 days, but within 90 days, only store
credit will be issued. No refunds or store credit are available after 90 days. Digital gift
cards and final-sale clearance items are non-refundable at any time. Refunds are processed to
the original payment method within 5-7 business days of the return being received at our
warehouse.

2. Shipping Policy
Standard shipping within the continental United States takes 5-7 business days and costs $6.99,
or is free on orders over $75. Expedited shipping (2-3 business days) costs $14.99. We currently
do not ship internationally. Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern Time ship the same business day;
orders placed after that cutoff ship the next business day. Tracking numbers are emailed
automatically once a label is generated.

3. Warranty
All Acme-branded tents, sleeping bags, and backpacks carry a limited lifetime warranty against
manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover normal wear and
tear, damage from misuse, or damage from improper storage. To file a warranty claim, contact
support with your order number and photos of the defect. Approved claims are resolved via
repair, replacement, or store credit at Acme's discretion.

4. Membership Program
The Acme Trailhead Rewards program is free to join. Members earn 1 point per dollar spent, and
100 points can be redeemed for a $5 discount. Members also receive early access to seasonal
sales and a free birthday gift. There is no paid tier of this program.

5. Contact Information
Customer support is available Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time, by email at
support@acmeoutdoor.example or by phone at 1-800-555-0199. Average email response time is
under 24 hours during business days.
